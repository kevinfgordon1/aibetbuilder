+EV Bets

+EV Bets is for comparing a sportsbook's price with a fair-odds estimate. Every available bet is ranked by expected value. True probability comes from the best opposing odds at matching lines among trusted books: DraftKings, FanDuel, Caesars, BetMGM, BetRivers, Fanatics, Hard Rock, theScore, Pinnacle, Kalshi, Novig, ProphetX, and Polymarket US. Kalshi, ProphetX, and Polymarket US prices are fee-adjusted. A filter narrows the list to a single sportsbook.

What EV means here

Expected value is what a bet is worth on average if you made it many times. A positive number means the book's price is better than the fair-odds estimate. Any single bet can still lose. The figure is an estimate, not a promise, and it moves when the odds move.

Where the fair price comes from

The fair price is the estimate of the real chance of winning after the sportsbook's margin is removed, using sharper books and exchanges. Promo Builder prices the other side of each leg with the inverse bet method. It walks Kalshi, Polymarket US, Novig, and ProphetX separately, on that venue's own book, after that venue's fee. In this pricing, Novig pregame quotes have no fee, the Polymarket US maker fee is $0, ProphetX is 2% of net winnings, and Kalshi uses its series fee.
