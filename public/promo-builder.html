Promo Builder

Promo Builder is the place to start on aibetbuilder. Pick your sportsbook and the promo you have — a profit boost, a free bet, or a no-sweat bet — and it searches leg combinations and ranks them by estimated value (EV). Boosted odds and fair odds are shown side by side. A boost is a sportsbook promo that raises your winnings. A free bet is site credit: if it wins you keep the profit, not the stake. A no-sweat bet is a cash bet that is refunded as site credit if it loses.

The inverse bet method

Promo Builder prices the other side of each leg with the inverse bet method. It walks Kalshi, Polymarket US, Novig, and ProphetX separately, on that venue's own book, after that venue's fee. In this pricing, Novig pregame quotes have no fee, the Polymarket US maker fee is $0, ProphetX is 2% of net winnings, and Kalshi uses its series fee. Venues are never added together, because a hedge is placed in one account. A venue that cannot fill the other side on its own is dropped, and the leg is priced at the best venue that can.

Kalshi

Kalshi prices in Promo Builder are net of Kalshi's series fee.

Polymarket US

Only Polymarket US is used. In Promo Builder's pricing, the Polymarket US maker fee is $0.

Novig

Novig pregame quotes are priced with no fee in Promo Builder.

ProphetX

ProphetX is priced after a 2% commission on net winnings.

What you do with a result

You place the sportsbook bet yourself. aibetbuilder does not log in to your book or send the bet. A 1-leg free bet can show the hedge that locks cash and the conversion rate. From a promo result you can open Combo Locks with that leg filled in; nothing is saved until you confirm. aibetbuilder is not a sportsbook. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank account. You place every bet yourself. Odds are collected from public sources and refreshed about every 5 minutes. Prices can be late, missing, or different from the number at your sportsbook. Check the live price and the promo rules before you bet. Tools and estimates only, not betting advice.
