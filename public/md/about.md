# Make your sportsbook promos actually pay.

AI Bet Builder is a free tool that helps you get the most out of your sportsbook promos. Tell it which sportsbook you use and what promo you have, like a profit boost or a free bet, and it shows you the bet where that promo is worth the most.


## What it does

- Promo Builder. Pick your sportsbook and promo. It checks thousands of bet combinations and ranks the ones where your boost or free bet is worth the most.
- +EV Bets. A list of bets where a sportsbook's price is better than the fair price, based on what other books and exchanges are offering.
- Odds Board. Compare prices for the same game across 20+ sportsbooks and exchanges in one place.


## How it works

We collect public odds from 20+ sportsbooks and exchanges, refreshed about every 5 minutes. We compare those prices to estimate each bet's fair chance of winning, then rank bets by how much value they offer.

You place every bet yourself, at your own sportsbook. AI Bet Builder is not a sportsbook. It doesn't take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank accounts.


## Who it's for

Anyone 21 or older who bets on sports where it's legal, whether you bet once a week or work every promo. It's free to use, and signing in is free too.

Odds change fast and no bet is a sure thing. Always check the price at your sportsbook before you bet, and only bet what you can afford to lose. If gambling stops being fun, call 1-800-GAMBLER.
