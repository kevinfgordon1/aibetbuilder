# Odds Board

The Odds Board compares prices for the same game across 20+ sportsbooks and exchanges in one place, with the best price highlighted. It covers moneylines (who wins), spreads (win by more than a number), and totals, plus championship futures with two-sided Yes/No prices from Kalshi and Polymarket US.


## What you can compare

- One game at a time, across the books and exchanges that are quoting it.
- Moneylines, spreads, and totals.
- Championship futures, including the Super Bowl, the College Football Playoff, the NBA Finals, the NCAAM champion, and the Stanley Cup.
- Leagues on the board: NFL, college football, NBA, college basketball, NHL, UFC, and soccer (EPL, MLS, and UEFA Champions League).


## How to read a price

The highlighted price is the best number the board currently has for that side. Exchange prices can include a fee adjustment, and a quote can be late or missing. The board reads public odds. It does not place a bet. aibetbuilder is not a sportsbook. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank account. You place every bet yourself. Odds are collected from public sources and refreshed about every 5 minutes. Prices can be late, missing, or different from the number at your sportsbook. Check the live price and the promo rules before you bet. Tools and estimates only, not betting advice. Results not guaranteed. 21+ only.
