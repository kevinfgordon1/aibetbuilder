# Learn the basics

New here? Start in [Promo Builder](/app?wizard=1#promo): pick your sportsbook and promo, and bets are ranked by estimated value. These are the terms you'll see.


## Terms you'll see


### EV (expected value)

What a bet is worth on average if you made it many times. +$20 EV on a $100 bet means it averages about $20 profit over the long run — any single bet can still lose.


### True Odds / fair odds

Our estimate of the real chance of winning, with the sportsbook's built-in margin (vig) removed, using sharper books and exchanges.


### Boost

A sportsbook promo that raises your winnings (e.g. 30% more profit). Boosts add the most value on the right legs — that's what Promo Builder finds.


### Free bet

Site credit you can bet with. If it wins you keep the profit, not the stake.


### No Sweat

A cash bet that is refunded as site credit if it loses.


### ML (moneyline)

Bet on who wins the game.


### SPR (spread)

Bet on a team to win by more than (or lose by less than) a set number of points, e.g. Knicks -3.5.


### TT (team total)

Bet on how many points one team scores, over or under a number, e.g. Celtics TT u125.5.


### Hedge / opposite side (opp)

Betting the other outcome somewhere else to reduce risk. “Best Opp” is the best price we found on that other side.


### Liquidity

How much money is available at a price on an exchange. “Hide low liquidity” removes bets where only small amounts can be placed.


### Depth

How big a bet the market can take at that price, shown as potential profit.


### American odds

-150 means bet $150 to win $100; +200 means bet $100 to win $200.
