# The aibetbuilder app

The app is where you run the tools. A new visitor can open Promo Builder with a short setup at /app?wizard=1#promo. Returning visitors land on the tools they were using. Sign in with Google, an email code or link, X, or Facebook. No credit card and no bank-account linking.


## Tabs

- Promo Builder ([what it does](/promo-builder)): [/app#promo](/app#promo). Ranks boosts, free bets, and no-sweat promos. Uses the inverse bet method on Kalshi, Polymarket US, Novig, and ProphetX, each with that venue's fee.
- Combo Locks ([what it does](/combo-locks)): [/app#combo](/app#combo). Beta. Hedges a parlay you already bet, on Kalshi and Polymarket US. Access is requested in the app.
- Odds Board ([what it does](/odds-board)): [/app#odds](/app#odds). Compares one game across books and exchanges.
- +EV Bets ([what it does](/ev-bets)): [/app#ev](/app#ev). Bets where a book's price beats the fair-odds estimate.
- Learn ([glossary](/learn)): the words used on the screen.

aibetbuilder is not a sportsbook. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank account. You place every bet yourself. Odds are collected from public sources and refreshed about every 5 minutes. Prices can be late, missing, or different from the number at your sportsbook. Check the live price and the promo rules before you bet. Tools and estimates only, not betting advice. Results not guaranteed. 21+ only.
