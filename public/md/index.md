# Find where your boost is worth the most.

aibetbuilder (AI Bet Builder) is a free tool that helps you get more out of sportsbook boosts, free bets, and no-sweat promos. Tell it which sportsbook you use and what you have, and it ranks bets by estimated value (EV) using live odds from 20+ sportsbooks and exchanges.

aibetbuilder is not a sportsbook. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank account. You place every bet yourself. Odds are collected from public sources and refreshed about every 5 minutes. Prices can be late, missing, or different from the number at your sportsbook. Check the live price and the promo rules before you bet. Tools and estimates only, not betting advice. Results not guaranteed. 21+ only.


## How it works

1. Tell us your promo. Pick your sportsbook and the boost, free bet, or no-sweat you got.
2. We rank the bets. Live odds from 20+ books and exchanges are compared, and bets are ranked by expected value, the estimated average result of each bet.
3. You place it yourself. Open your sportsbook and place the bet. aibetbuilder never touches your account or your money.


## Tools


### Promo Builder

Start here. Enter a boost or free bet and see which bets make it worth the most. Promo Builder prices the other side of each leg with the inverse bet method. It walks Kalshi, Polymarket US, Novig, and ProphetX separately, on that venue's own book, after that venue's fee. In this pricing, Novig pregame quotes have no fee, the Polymarket US maker fee is $0, ProphetX is 2% of net winnings, and Kalshi uses its series fee. A 1-leg free bet can also show the hedge that locks cash, and the conversion rate.

Read the [Promo Builder](/promo-builder) page, or open it in the app at [/app?wizard=1#promo](/app?wizard=1#promo).


### Combo Locks

Combo Locks is in beta. Submit a parlay you have already bet at your sportsbook, and it hedges that parlay on Kalshi and Polymarket US. You set your stake, your odds, and the odds you are willing to sell at. The same parlay is offered to traders at your price. As the hedge fills, more of it is hedged. You can pause a lock or stop everything with the master switch. Access is requested inside the app. Real prices and fills vary.

Read [how Combo Locks works](/combo-locks). In the app it opens at [/app#combo](/app#combo).


### Odds Board

The Odds Board compares one game's prices across sportsbooks and exchanges, with the best price highlighted. It covers moneylines, spreads, and totals, plus championship futures with two-sided prices from Kalshi and Polymarket US.

Read the [Odds Board](/odds-board) page, or open [/app#odds](/app#odds).


### +EV Bets

+EV Bets lists bets where a book's price beats aibetbuilder's fair-odds estimate. True probability comes from the best opposing odds at matching lines among trusted books (DraftKings, FanDuel, Caesars, BetMGM, BetRivers, Fanatics, Hard Rock, theScore, Pinnacle, Kalshi, Novig, ProphetX, Polymarket US). Kalshi, ProphetX, and Polymarket US prices are fee-adjusted.

Read the [+EV Bets](/ev-bets) page, or open [/app#ev](/app#ev).


## Leagues on the boards
