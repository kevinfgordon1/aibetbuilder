# Combo Locks

Combo Locks is in beta. Submit a parlay you have already bet at your sportsbook, and Combo Locks hedges it on Kalshi and Polymarket US. Hedge most or all of it to lock in a result whether the parlay wins or loses. Real prices and fills vary, and a lock is not a promise that the hedge will fill.


## How a lock works

1. Place a boosted or free-bet parlay at your sportsbook, such as DraftKings or FanDuel.
2. Add the same parlay in Combo Locks with your stake, your odds, and the odds you are willing to sell it at.
3. aibetbuilder offers that parlay to traders on Kalshi and Polymarket US at your price. The offer is priced off the best opposing price across prediction markets. You can pause any lock, or stop everything with the master switch.
4. When a trader takes it, that much of the parlay is hedged. Quoting means it is looking for a buyer. Partly filled means some of it is hedged. Filled means the profit on that lock is locked. Paused or Stopped means nothing is being offered.
5. After the games end, the lock moves to History with the profit or loss.


## Access

Signed-out visitors and accounts that have not been approved see a request screen. Sign in, then request access, and the page opens for that account if the request is approved. A declined request can be sent again. aibetbuilder is not a sportsbook. It does not take bets, hold money, or connect to your sportsbook or bank account. You place every bet yourself. Odds are collected from public sources and refreshed about every 5 minutes. Prices can be late, missing, or different from the number at your sportsbook. Check the live price and the promo rules before you bet. Tools and estimates only, not betting advice. Results not guaranteed. 21+ only.
