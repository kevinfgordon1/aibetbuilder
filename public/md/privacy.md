# Your data, in plain English

AI Bet Builder ("we", "us") runs aibetbuilder.io. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: we keep only what we need to sign you in and run the site, we never sell your data, and we never ask for bank or payment details. Last updated October 7, 2026. The HTML page is the full policy.


## What we collect

You can sign in with Google, X, Facebook, or a one-time link sent to your email. Sign-in is handled by Supabase. When you sign in we store your email address, your name and profile photo if the service you sign in with shares them, which service you used, and that service's account ID for you. We never see or store your Google, X, or Facebook password. If you use more than one of these with the same email, they are linked to one account.

When you use the site we store settings such as favorite sports and your default sportsbook, and activity while signed in (tabs, filters, and bets you open) with your account ID and email. Anything you enter in an invite-only tool, if you have access to one, is stored with your account. We don't collect bank accounts, cards, or payment details on this policy's terms. We don't collect sportsbook logins. We don't post to X or Facebook for you.


## How we use it and who we share with

We use the data to sign you in, remember settings, understand how the site is used, and reply when you contact us. We don't sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising. We share it only with the services that run the site: Supabase (sign-in and storage), Vercel (hosting), Google Analytics, and Microsoft Clarity. Google, X, or Facebook know you used them to sign in if you chose one of them. We may share information if the law requires it.


## Analytics

Cookies and local storage keep you signed in and remember preferences. Google Analytics records pages, buttons, device, browser, and approximate location. Microsoft Clarity records clicks, scrolling, and mouse movement. You can block cookies; you may need to sign in again.


## Data deletion

To delete your account and data, email [support@aibetbuilder.io](mailto:support@aibetbuilder.io) with the subject "Delete my account", from the email on the account if you can. We'll delete the account, saved settings, and activity history within 30 days and email you when it's done. You can also remove AI Bet Builder's access in Facebook, X, or Google settings. Removing access stops future sign-ins through that service but doesn't delete data we already have. You can ask for a copy of your data or a correction by email. The site is only for people 21 and older.
