Account and sign-in

You can sign in with Google, X, Facebook, or a code sent to your email. If a code or link does not work, request a new one on the sign-in screen and open the site in Safari or Chrome. For help getting into an account, or to ask for a copy of your data or to delete it, email us from the address on the account when you can. We don't promise that a sign-in will succeed.

Billing and subscriptions

If you have a question about a charge, a receipt, or a subscription, email us from the address on the account and describe what you see. We'll look into it and reply. We don't quote prices here, and we don't promise a refund, a credit, or any other outcome.

A price or odds number looks wrong

Email us the sport, the teams, the sportsbook, and about when you saw the number. Prices on the site can be late, missing, or different from the price at your sportsbook. We don't promise a correction, a result, or that a number will match your book. Check the live price at your sportsbook before you bet. Nothing on this page is betting advice.
