Privacy Policy

Your data, in plain English

Last updated October 7, 2026

AI Bet Builder ("we", "us") runs aibetbuilder.io. This page explains what we collect, why, and how to get it deleted. The short version: we keep only what we need to sign you in and run the site, we never sell your data, and we never ask for bank or payment details.

What we collect

When you sign in

You can sign in with Google, X, Facebook, or a one-time link sent to your email. Sign-in is handled by our login provider, Supabase. When you sign in we store:

  • Your email address
  • Your name and profile photo (if the service you sign in with shares them)
  • Which service you used (Google, X, Facebook or email) and that service's account ID for you

If you sign in with more than one of these using the same email address, they're linked to one AI Bet Builder account. We never see or store your Google, X or Facebook password.

When you use the site

  • Your settings, like your favorite sports and default sportsbook, saved to your account so they follow you between devices.
  • Activity while signed in, like which tabs and filters you use and which bets you open. It's stored with your account ID and email and helps us see what's useful and fix what's broken.
  • Anything you enter in invite-only tools, if you've been given access to one, is stored with your account.

What we don't collect

  • No bank accounts, cards or payment details. The site is free and takes no payments.
  • No sportsbook logins or account linking. You place bets yourself, at your own sportsbook.
  • No posting on your behalf. We never post to X or Facebook for you.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep you signed in
  • To remember your settings
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it and fix problems
  • To reply when you contact us

We don't sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising.

Analytics, cookies & local storage

We use cookies and your browser's local storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences, like your sportsbook choices and how the odds board is laid out.

We also use two analytics tools to see how people use the site, whether or not you're signed in:

  • Google Analytics records pages viewed, buttons used, device and browser type, and approximate location. You can opt out with Google's opt-out browser add-on. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how you interact with pages, like clicks, scrolling and mouse movement, so we can spot confusing parts of the site. See the Microsoft privacy statement.

Both tools use cookies. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The site still works, but you may need to sign in again.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the services that run the site for us, and only what they need:

  • Supabase handles sign-in and stores your account and settings.
  • Vercel hosts the website.
  • Google (Analytics) and Microsoft (Clarity) provide the analytics described above.
  • Google, X or Facebook know you used them to sign in to AI Bet Builder, if you chose one of them.

We may also share information if the law requires it. If you copy a link to a pick and send it to someone, they'll see that pick, but not your account details.

Data deletion

To delete your account and data, email kev120909@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Send it from the email address on your account if you can. If you signed in with X or Facebook, include your X handle or Facebook name so we can find your account.

We'll delete your account, your saved settings and your activity history within 30 days and email you when it's done. Basic records kept by our analytics tools aren't tied to your name or email and expire on their own.

You can also cut off AI Bet Builder's access to your sign-in account at any time:

  • Facebook: Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites → remove AI Bet Builder.
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps → AI Bet Builder → Revoke app permissions.
  • Google: your Google Account → Security → Your connections to third-party apps & services → AI Bet Builder → Delete all connections.

Removing access stops future sign-ins through that service but doesn't delete the data we already have, so email us too if you want it deleted.

Your other choices

You can ask us for a copy of your data or ask us to correct it by emailing kev120909@gmail.com. You can use most of the site without signing in at all.

Age

AI Bet Builder is only for people 21 and older. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you think someone under 21 has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
